Weather forecasts provided by Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com/) using NOAA GFS through its Previous Runs API. This folder uses previous_day1, values predicted 24 hours before valid time, not target-hour observations/reanalysis. Source URL, units, model and lead time are in source.json.
